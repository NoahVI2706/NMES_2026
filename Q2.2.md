# Q2.2 - Geometry simplification

The question asks whether the cable geometry can be simplified without degrading the precision of the result.

The cable is represented by a **2D cross-section** rather than by a full 3D cable model. This is appropriate because the cable is assumed to be uniform along its length and the electric-field solution is wanted in the transverse plane. The following additional simplifications are made:

* The stranded aluminium conductor is replaced by a homogeneous solid conductor with an effective radius of 9.05 mm.
* The conductor, inner screen, XLPE insulation, outer screen and lead sheath are treated as concentric circular layers.
* The actual cable stranding, gaps and individual strand shapes are ignored.
* The individual phase conductors are placed at positions representing a three-core cable; a one-core configuration can be selected with the `NumberOfCores` parameter.
* Armour and filler materials are omitted.
* Longitudinal variations along the cable are not modelled.

The supplied cable dimensions are preserved where they affect the electric-field region: conductor diameter, inner-screen thickness, 8 mm XLPE insulation thickness, outer-screen thickness and 1.5 mm lead-sheath thickness. The XLPE relative permittivity is set to 2.3.

These simplifications should not remove the main radial voltage gradient, so they are expected to have little effect on the fundamental capacitance and electric-field calculation. The simplification study should compare at least:

1. the solid conductor with stranding ignored;
2. the conductor removed from the computational mesh;
3. the lead-sheath thickness set to zero.

The results should be considered sufficiently precise when the field distribution and capacitance change remains small between the detailed and simplified models.
