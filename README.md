# NMES_2026

The objective of the lab sessions is to numerically characterise a power cable system using electric,
magnetic and coupled magneto-thermal finite element (FE) models. Power cables play a key role in
transmission and distribution networks. Their rating is limited by the electric stress in the insulation
and, above all, by the maximum admissible conductor temperature, which depends on the losses in
the conductors and in the metallic sheaths, armour and pipe. Numerical simulations help to design
the cable system, predict ageing and degradation, and prevent failures. The lab follows the approach
of [1], but you will work on a cable of your own choice, and verify and validate your results against
analytical estimates and the cable datasheet.
By the end of the project, you should be able to: translate a real device and its datasheet into a
well-posed, bounded 2D FE model and justify every simplifying assumption; implement and couple FE
formulations in GetDP/Gmsh; verify (mesh convergence, analytical checks) and validate (datasheet)
your results; use your model to propose and quantify design improvements; and present and defend
your modelling choices orally
